# Conveyer Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Conveyer is a program that plays Factorio. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Models.** Game state is sent to the model that plays, either a local model on your machine or Anthropic's API if you configure a key.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Conveyer isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
